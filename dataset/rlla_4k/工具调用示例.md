## 示例 1: `Get Driver Standings After a Race`

### 用户查询

```
**Dialogue Records History**
<user> I need to know the driver standings after the 7th race of 2021 and also after the 8th race of 2021. </user>
```

### 工具描述

```
Description: Fetches the driver standings after a specific race in a season.
Parameters: {"year": {"description": "The year of the season", "type": "int", "default": ""}, "round": {"description": "The round number of the race", "type": "int", "default": ""}}
```

### 工具调用

```json
{"name": "Get Driver Standings After a Race", "parameters": {"year": "2021", "round": "8"}
```
